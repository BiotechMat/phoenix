# Chief Executive Officer

- **Seat held by:** Mat Beale, founder
- **Reports to:** The company's shareholders; today, the founder
- **Direct reports:** CIO, Head of Quantitative Research, CRO, CTO, Head of
  Execution and Trading, CFO, CCO

## Purpose of the role

Set what Phoenix is for and decide how much of the company's capital it may
put at risk, on what terms, and when. Own every decision with capital,
a limit, a venue, legal exposure or the move from paper to live attached.
Hold the officers to the charter's standard of evidence: net numbers,
labelled stages, cited sources, dissent on the record.

## Background the role calls for

- Working knowledge of the system as built: the CEO wrote or directed the
  sleeves, the research pipeline, the risk committee and the control centre
  (`PROJECT.md`), and can read a CPCV tournament result or a ledger entry
  without an intermediary.
- Enough market knowledge to judge a thesis: how perpetual funding works,
  what a liquidation cascade does to a leveraged book, why a post-only
  order may never fill, why a Sharpe ratio from a backtest with many trials
  is not a Sharpe ratio.
- The discipline to let the CRO's and CCO's stops hold, and to record an
  override as a decision rather than route around it.
- A clear view of the company's capital as the only capital: no outside
  money, no clients, no public claims, so that a loss is the founder's and
  nobody else's.

## Responsibilities

1. Decide strategy: the return target, the drawdown the company will carry,
   the asset universe and the sleeve line-up; record each in the register
   with options, reasoning and dissent.
2. Decide the capital base and every live tranche, on the CFO's, CRO's and
   CCO's advice; set the loss limit at which trading stops and the board
   reconvenes.
3. Decide every limit (leverage, position, concentration, drawdown, kill
   switch) on the CRO's proposal; never raise one without the CRO's memo
   and a recorded decision.
4. Decide the move from paper to live and every promotion of a model to
   live, on the gates in charter section 6.
5. Approve every venue and every recurring cost.
6. Allocate the founder's time between research, engineering, operations
   and the legal work, with the CFO's costing of that time in view.
7. Convene the board on any question that crosses two remits, and consult a
   single officer on anything inside one.
8. Resolve disagreements between officers; where the CEO overrides a CRO or
   CCO stop, record the override as a decision with the reasoning.

## Decision rights

Final on everything. The charter's decision-rights table (section 4) lists
who must be consulted before each class of decision.

## Immediate priorities

1. State the return target and the maximum drawdown as numbers (Q-01).
2. Decide the first live tranche and its loss limit in principle, so the
   CFO, CRO and CCO can work towards the "first live capital" gate (Q-02).
3. Confirm the CEO's jurisdiction to the CCO so the venue-terms review can
   be done against the right regulator (Q-03).
4. Decide whether the paper stage runs for a fixed minimum period before
   any live capital, and how long (Q-04).
