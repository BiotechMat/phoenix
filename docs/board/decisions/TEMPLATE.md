# D-NNNN: Title in one line

- **Date:** D Month YYYY
- **Decided by:** CEO
- **Status:** Decided | Superseded by D-NNNN
- **Consulted:** seats consulted
- **Kind:** Strategy | Limit | Tranche | Venue | Promotion | Override of a stop | Governance | Other

## Decision

One paragraph stating what was decided, in terms that can be checked later.
A limit or tranche is stated as a number with its unit; a venue by name; a
promotion by the champion artefact's hash.

## Context

Why the question arose. Cite the documents, code, ledger figures or
tournament results that frame it, each labelled by stage (backtest,
tournament, paper, live).

## Options considered

1. Option, with its main consequence.
2. Option, with its main consequence.

## Reasoning

Why this option. State which facts are verified and which are estimates,
and which stops, if any, the decision overrides.

## Dissent

Which officers disagreed, and their reasoning, recorded as they gave it.
A CRO or CCO stop that was overridden is recorded here in full.
"None recorded" if no officer dissented.

## Consequences

What changes because of this decision: code to change (limits, parameters,
contracts), records to update, gates that now apply, what the next review
checks and when.
