`ARCHITECTURE_STATE.md`, `TEST_INFRA.md`, `TEST_READY.md`,

work touches them. A question closes when a decision in the register
resolves it. Every number here is labelled by stage; nothing in this file
is a live result, because nothing has traded live.

## Risks

| Id | Risk | Owner | Mitigation in place or proposed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-01 | Backtest-to-live gap: a model passes the CPCV gates on history and loses live because of overfitting the trial count hid, regime change, or costs the labels understated. | HoR | Gates with DSR and PBO in `research/validation/cpcv_gatekeeper.py`; trial count to be made auditable (research log); CRO's independent validation; paper stage before live (Q-04); fee hurdle tied to the HoE's verified costs (Q-16). |
| R-02 | Liquidation cascade: a leveraged position on Hyperliquid is liquidated during a correlated move, with the loss amplified by the cascade the alpha sleeve is meant to detect. | CRO | 2.5x gross cap in code; distance-to-liquidation floor and drawdown limits to be added (Q-17); stress programme at current and proposed limits. |
| R-03 | Venue outage or API drift mid-position: Hyperliquid or Bybit changes a schema, rate-limits or goes down with positions open and the failover does not pick them up. | CTO | Bybit failover in `core/data_ingestion.py` for data; execution failover and venue reconciliation on restart to be built and tested (Q-18). |
| R-04 | Key compromise or loss: a live agent key is leaked (committed, logged) or lost, and funds are traded away or stranded. | CTO | No key in the repository today; custody policy (Q-07) with trade-only permissions before any live key; secret scan in CI (Q-12). |
| R-05 | Model and daemon drift: the LLM risk committee is nondeterministic, returns malformed output, or its neutral fallback silently sets $M_{\text{regime}}$ in a way nobody notices. | HoR | Strict Pydantic validation and deterministic neutral fallback in `sentiment/mlx_risk_daemon.py`; ablation (Q-05); alerting on fallback rate (CTO). |
| R-06 | Regulatory or venue-terms breach: the company trades where it may not, or in a way the venue prohibits, and the account is closed or funds forfeited. | CCO | Jurisdiction position (Q-03) and venue terms (Q-13) before live; CCO stop on live capital and new venues. |
